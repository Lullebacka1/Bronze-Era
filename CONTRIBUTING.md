Anyone can help by submitting a pull request. The development staff will review each contribution and decide whether it should be accepted.

## Rules Before Submitting a Pull Request

* Test your changes before submitting them. They must not cause any crashes.
* Try to avoid bugs and make sure your additions work correctly.
* Follow the mod’s existing style, structure, and organization whenever possible.
* Keep your content historically coherent. Some creative freedom is allowed, as gameplay and fun remain the priority.
* Clearly explain the changes made in your Pull Request.
* Be respectful toward other contributors and the development team. Basic rules of good conduct apply.
* The development team may accept, request changes to, or reject any contribution.

## Contributors

Active contributors will receive the **Helper** role on our Discord server. This role grants access to a special channel where Helpers can communicate directly with the developers.
Regular and trusted contributors may later be invited to officially join the development team, with additional access and responsibilities.
